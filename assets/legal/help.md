# Help and support

Mova is designed to work without an account and without a connection.

## Getting started

- Choose a training plan during onboarding.
- Open Today to see the next session, your steps, and your streak.
- Start a workout from Today or Train and log each set as you go.
- Review history, personal records, and progress from the Progress tab.
- Open Me to edit your profile and open Settings.

## Units

Settings has separate controls for weight, distance, and height. Values are stored as kilograms, kilometers, and centimeters on the device and converted for display. Changing units does not change the underlying data.

## Workout reminders

Turn on Workout reminders in Settings, choose the days and time, and allow notifications when your device asks. Reminders are scheduled locally. If a reminder does not appear, check that notifications are allowed for Mova in your device settings and that a reminder day is selected.

## Steps and health

You can enter steps manually from the Today steps card. To read steps from your device, connect Apple Health or Health Connect in Settings. Mova reads step count only. If health access is unavailable, manual entry still works.

## Sample posts

The Social tab starts with three clearly labeled sample posts. Use Hide sample posts in Settings to remove them. Your own posts stay on the device.

## Reset local data

Settings has Reset local data. This deletes the profile, plan, routines, history, personal records, progress, steps, feed posts, and preferences from this device and returns the app to the welcome screen. The reset cannot be undone.

## Data and privacy

Mova has no account and no cloud sync. Read the Privacy policy in Settings for details about local storage, health access, notifications, backups, and deletion.

## Contact

For help, use the support contact listed in the app store listing. Include your device model, operating system version, and a short description of the issue. Do not send health records or other sensitive personal data.
