# Mova privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Mova is a local-first fitness app. This policy explains what data the app stores, where it lives, and what control you have.

## What Mova stores

Mova stores the information you enter while using the app:

- Guest profile details such as name, experience level, goals, gender, birth date, height, and body weight.
- Training plan choices, routines, workout history, personal records, streaks, and progress entries.
- Daily step counts, either entered manually or read from your device health store with your permission.
- App preferences such as units, reminder days and time, privacy choices, and sample feed dismissal.
- Social feed posts and kudos created inside the app.

## Where data lives

All app data is stored locally on your device using the operating system app storage. Mova has no account system, no cloud sync, and no server that receives your data.

Mova does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reports, or location data. Mova does not transmit your profile, workouts, health data, or feed to any third party.

## Health data

Health access is optional. If you connect Apple Health or Health Connect, Mova requests read access to step count only. Mova reads steps to show your daily total and never writes health data.

Health data is processed on your device and is not transmitted off the device. You can disconnect health access at any time in Mova Settings or in your device health settings. Disconnecting stops future reads but does not delete step entries already stored by Mova.

## Notifications

Workout reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Mova does not use a remote push service. Notification permission is optional, and you can disable reminders in Mova Settings or in your device notification settings.

## Social feed

The Social feed in Mova is a local sample experience. Posts and kudos stay on your device. No content is shared with other people or uploaded to a service.

## Backups

Mova disables Android cloud backup for its app data. On iOS, device backups are controlled by your operating system and your iCloud or computer backup settings. A device backup may include Mova data if you have enabled backups at the operating system level.

## Deleting your data

Settings includes Reset local data. Confirming the reset deletes the Mova profile, plan, routines, workout history, personal records, progress, steps, feed posts, units, reminders, and privacy choices from this device. Notifications scheduled by Mova are cancelled. The app returns to the welcome screen with fresh local defaults.

There is no account to delete and no server copy to request.

## Children

Mova is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version ships inside the app with a new last updated date.

## Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the support contact listed in the app store listing.
