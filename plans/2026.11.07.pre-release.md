## 1. 🎯 Scope

This plan covers everything required to turn the completed app into a submitted 1.0.0 release:

- Version control baseline and release tag.
- Android upload signing and signed app bundle.
- Apple distribution signing, archive, and IPA.
- Google Play Console and App Store Connect setup.
- Health data declarations and store privacy forms.
- Legal review, policy URLs, and brand sign-off.
- Store screenshots and listing metadata.
- Physical iOS and Android device QA, including VoiceOver and TalkBack.
- Signed release builds, submission, review fixes, and release verification.

This plan does not add product features. Any feature work needs a new plan.

## 5. 🚦 Release gate

The release gate runs after PRE-17 and requires:

```bash
flutter pub get
dart format --output=none --set-exit-if-changed lib test
flutter analyze
flutter test
flutter test --coverage
tool/check_coverage.sh coverage/lcov.info
flutter build apk --debug
flutter build apk --release
flutter build appbundle --release
flutter build ipa --release
flutter build web --release
```

Also required:

- Physical iOS and Android QA records in `store/release-notes.md`.
- Signed artifact checksums in `store/release-notes.md`.
- Approved legal, brand, and store listings.
- Both store submissions approved or in staged rollout.
- `CHANGELOG.md` contains only compact completed-work entries.
- No `TODO` or `FIXME` in `lib/`, and no em dash in `lib/`, `test/`, or user-facing assets.

## 7. ⚠️ Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Physical hardware is unavailable | High | Keep PRE-11 and PRE-12 open; do not claim hardware verification |
| Store review rejects health or privacy copy | High | Prepare declarations before submission and keep the manual steps fallback documented |
| Signing credentials are lost | High | Keep the keystore and passwords in the user's password manager with a backup |
| Screenshot or listing drift from the shipped build | Medium | Capture screenshots from the exact signed build |
| Store review finds a crash on a device not covered locally | Medium | Use internal testing and staged rollout before full production |
| Release build differs from the tested candidate | Medium | Record checksums, rebuild only from the tagged commit, and re-run the gate |
